What is it

Swapping tokens on Mantle

An Ethereum layer-2 with its own gas token

A Mantle swap exchanges one token for another on Mantle, a layer-2 network that settles to Ethereum. Mantle uses a modular design and its native token, MNT, is the gas token, so network fees are paid in MNT rather than ETH.

What people trade here

Common trades are MNT against USDC or USDT, stablecoin to stablecoin, and MNT against ETH. A DEX or aggregator searches the available pools and shows a quote before you sign.

Mantle vs Ethereum

How Mantle differs from Ethereum mainnet

The swap flow looks the same, but a few details change because you are on a layer-2 with a different gas token.

Same address, different network

Your wallet address works on both, but balances are separate. Tokens on Mantle must be moved there first before you can swap them.

How to swap

How to swap on Mantle

Quote first, sign second

The interface shows the route, expected output, price impact and slippage tolerance. Nothing is sent until you confirm in your wallet.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet and switch it to the Mantle network.
  2. Pick the token to sell and the token to buy, then enter an amount.
  3. Review the quote, route and estimated fee in MNT.
  4. Approve the sell token once if it is an ERC-20 and this is its first swap from this wallet.
  5. Sign the swap; the bought token arrives in your wallet after confirmation.
Fees & gas

What a Mantle swap costs

Network fee in MNT

Every approval and swap is a transaction paid in MNT. Fees on a layer-2 are usually much lower than on Ethereum mainnet but still move with network use. The Mantle docs explain the fee model.

Trading cost

The quoted amount already reflects pool fees, price impact and any aggregator fee. Smaller pools shift the price more on large orders.

Keep some MNT

Holding only USDC or USDT leaves you unable to pay gas. Keep a little MNT in the wallet before you swap.

Security

Staying safe on Mantle

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction. This interface routes and quotes; it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify token contract addresses; lookalike tokens are common on newer networks.
  • Confirm your wallet is on Mantle before approving anything.
  • Approve only what you need and revoke old allowances.
  • Check price impact and slippage, especially on thin pools.
  • Smart contracts and bridges can fail, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Mantle Swap FAQ

What is Mantle Swap?

It is a non-custodial interface for swapping tokens such as MNT, USDC and USDT on the Mantle network. You connect your own wallet, review the quote and sign each transaction yourself.

What is Mantle?

Mantle is an Ethereum layer-2 network with a modular design that settles to Ethereum. Its native token, MNT, is used to pay gas.

Which token pays gas on Mantle?

Gas on Mantle is paid in MNT, not ETH. Keep a small MNT balance so you can approve and sign swaps.

How do I get tokens onto Mantle?

Move them with a bridge from Ethereum or another chain, or withdraw to Mantle from an exchange that supports it. Swaps only work with tokens that are already on Mantle in your wallet.

Why do I need to approve a token first?

ERC-20 tokens like USDC and USDT need a one-time allowance so the router can move them for the swap. Native MNT needs no approval. The approval is its own transaction and costs a little gas.

Can the amount I receive differ from the quote?

Yes, if the price moves before your transaction confirms. Your slippage tolerance sets a floor, and the swap reverts if the result would fall below it.
