Swap Mantle from your wallet: start to finish

  1. Choose your MNT route

    Open the app, select MNT and enter an amount. Match the sending network to where your MNT is held: Mantle Network and Ethereum are different networks.

  2. Review the quote and address

    Choose your receiving asset and network. Check the final amount, minimum deposit, fees and quote expiry, then enter a compatible receiving address.

  3. Send MNT and track delivery

    Follow the deposit instructions exactly. The service releases the receiving asset after your Mantle transaction confirms and the order is processed. Keep your order ID and transaction hash.

Mantle swap fees: network, service and receiving-chain costs

Low L2 gas is one part of the cost. Compare the final receiving amount for the same MNT amount and destination network.

01 / Sending-network gas

Mantle transactions use MNT for gas. Keep some MNT available beyond the amount you send. MNT held on Ethereum requires ETH for gas.

02 / Swap cost

The service fee and exchange-rate spread affect your quote. On-chain routes can also involve liquidity-provider fees and slippage.

03 / Receiving-network costs

Delivery or cross-chain costs depend on the route. Read the quote’s fee breakdown before confirming.

Mantle swap sent but not received? What to check

Not enough gas

Reduce the MNT amount to leave room for the wallet’s gas estimate. On Ethereum, top up ETH instead.

Quote expired or changed

Refresh the quote before sending. Recheck the deposit minimum, exact amount and destination details.

Swap pending or missing

Check the deposit hash first. If confirmed, use the order ID to check processing. Verify the receiving network before looking for the token in your wallet.

Mantle swap essentials: minimums, rates and receiving funds

Where can I swap Mantle to USDT?

Open the swap app, select MNT as the sending asset and USDT as the receiving asset. Check the available sending and receiving networks, then review the quote before depositing.

Do I need MNT or ETH for gas to swap Mantle?

On Mantle Network, transaction gas is paid in MNT. If your MNT is an ERC-20 token on Ethereum, you need ETH for Ethereum gas. Select the network that actually holds your balance.

How long does it take to swap Mantle?

Total time depends on deposit confirmations, service processing and the receiving network. Check the order’s delivery estimate; fast L2 confirmation does not determine the full swap time.

Can I swap Mantle to ETH on Ethereum?

Select ETH and Ethereum as the receiving asset and network when that route is available. An MNT-to-ETH quote alone does not identify the destination chain; check it before sending.

Why does the receiving amount change when I swap Mantle?

A floating quote follows market prices until the rate is set. Route fees and liquidity also affect the final amount. Refresh an expired quote and review any slippage or fixed-rate conditions before confirming.
